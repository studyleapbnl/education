Section 01

Introduction & Acceptance

These B2B Partnership Terms & Conditions ("Partnership Terms") govern the referral partnership between Study LEAP (also known as "Study LEAP"), a financial consultancy registered under UDYAM Registration No. UDYAM-PB-XX-XXXXXXX (pending update), with registered office at SCO No 5, Behind New Bus Stand, Barnala, Punjab – 148101, India ("Study LEAP", "we", "us"), and any business or individual that applies for and is approved into our B2B Partnership Program ("Partner", "you").

By submitting a partnership application, signing a partnership agreement referencing these Partnership Terms, or referring any client to Study LEAP under the Program, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be legally bound by these Partnership Terms, in addition to our general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with any part of these Partnership Terms, please do not apply to or participate in the Program.
Section 02

Definitions

  • "Program" means Study LEAP's B2B Partnership / referral program described on the B2B Partnership page.
  • "Referral" means an introduction, by the Partner to Study LEAP, of a prospective client seeking an education loan.
  • "Referred Client" means an individual referred by the Partner who is contacted by Study LEAP and considered for loan facilitation services.
  • "Qualified Referral" means a Referred Client who proceeds to loan disbursement through Study LEAP, as defined further in Section 08.
  • "Commission" means the referral fee payable to the Partner for each Qualified Referral, as set out in Section 08.
  • "Confidential Information" means any non-public business, client, or commercial information exchanged between the parties under the Program.
Section 03

Partner Eligibility & Onboarding

The Program is open to businesses and individuals who can responsibly refer prospective students, including but not limited to:

  • Study-abroad and education consultants
  • IELTS/TOEFL/PTE coaching institutes and test-prep centres
  • Colleges, universities, and their placement/admissions cells
  • Visa and immigration consultants
  • Independent financial advisors and loan DSAs (Direct Selling Agents)
  • Corporate HR teams offering education-financing benefits to employees

All applicants must complete the partnership application form, provide basic business verification (e.g. business registration, GST number, or equivalent proof of operation), and be approved in writing by Study LEAP before referring any client. Study LEAP reserves the right to accept or decline any application at its sole discretion, without obligation to state a reason.

Section 04

Scope of the Partnership

This is a non-exclusive referral arrangement. The Partner's role is limited to introducing prospective clients to Study LEAP. Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

  • The Partner is not authorised to negotiate loan terms, quote interest rates, promise approval, or make representations on Study LEAP's behalf.
  • Study LEAP retains sole and complete discretion over whether to accept, process, or decline any Referral, and over how any loan facilitation is carried out.
  • The Partner may enter into similar referral arrangements with other businesses, and Study LEAP may run similar programs with other partners, unless an exclusivity arrangement has been separately agreed in writing.
Section 05

Referral Process

Referrals must be submitted through the method(s) designated by Study LEAP from time to time (e.g. the partner inquiry form, a shared referral link, or a dedicated partner portal/WhatsApp line). For each Referral, the Partner must:

  • Have the Referred Client's consent to share their name and contact details with Study LEAP (see Section 09)
  • Provide accurate contact information for the Referred Client
  • Avoid duplicate submission of a client already in Study LEAP's active pipeline

Study LEAP will make reasonable efforts to contact each Referred Client within a target timeframe (e.g. 2 business days) and will keep the Partner informed of high-level status where practical.

Section 06

Partner Obligations

  • Represent Study LEAP's services accurately and honestly; do not guarantee loan approval, specific interest rates, or disbursement timelines to any Referred Client
  • Do not misrepresent yourself as an employee, agent, or authorised representative of Study LEAP, or as a bank/NBFC representative
  • Obtain the Referred Client's informed consent before sharing their personal information with Study LEAP
  • Comply with all applicable laws, including consumer-protection and data-protection law, in how you source and handle Referrals
  • Do not use Study LEAP's name, logo, or brand assets except as permitted under Section 10
  • Promptly notify Study LEAP of any complaint received from a Referred Client relating to the referral process
Section 07

Study LEAP's Obligations

  • Process each Qualified Referral in line with our standard service quality, at no cost to the Referred Client for consultation
  • Treat Referred Clients' data in accordance with our Privacy Policy
  • Pay Commission owed under Section 08 accurately and on the agreed schedule
  • Provide the Partner with reasonable marketing collateral and program support materials on request
  • Notify the Partner of material changes to the Program, including to the Commission structure, with reasonable advance notice

Data Protection & Confidentiality

Intellectual Property & Use of Brand

Study LEAP's name, logo, and marketing materials remain the exclusive property of Study LEAP. The Partner may use approved Study LEAP marketing collateral solely to promote the Program, and only in the form provided. Any co-branded material, press mention, or use of the Study LEAP name in the Partner's own marketing requires prior written approval from Study LEAP.

Section 11

Independent Contractor Relationship

Nothing in these Partnership Terms creates an employment, agency, joint-venture, or partnership-in-law relationship between Study LEAP and the Partner. The Partner acts as an independent contractor and has no authority to bind Study LEAP to any obligation, make representations on its behalf beyond what is permitted in Section 04, or incur any liability in Study LEAP's name.

Section 12

Regulatory Compliance

Where the Partner's activities under this Program resemble those of a Direct Selling Agent (DSA) for a financial product, the Partner agrees to conduct itself in line with applicable RBI guidance on outsourcing and DSA conduct, including not misrepresenting loan terms, not charging Referred Clients any fee for the introduction, and cooperating with Study LEAP's compliance reviews on request.

Section 13

Term & Termination

The partnership begins on the date of written approval by Study LEAP and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate this arrangement at any time, for any reason, with [14/30 days — confirm] written notice. Study LEAP may terminate immediately, without notice, if the Partner breaches Sections 06, 09, or 10, or misrepresents Study LEAP to a client.

Commission for Qualified Referrals that disbursed before the termination date remains payable per Section 08, even if paid out after termination. No Commission accrues for Referrals submitted after the termination date.

Limitation of Liability & Indemnification

Study LEAP's total liability arising from this Program is limited to Commission actually owed and unpaid to the Partner. Study LEAP is not liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential loss arising from the Partner's participation in the Program.

The Partner agrees to indemnify Study LEAP against any claim, loss, or liability arising from the Partner's misrepresentation to a Referred Client, breach of these Partnership Terms, or sharing of client data without valid consent.
